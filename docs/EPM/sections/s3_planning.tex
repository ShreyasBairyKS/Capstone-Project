\section{Project Planning}
\subsection{Project Objective}
Deliver the VisionQAI inspection system, comprising cap, fill-level and label anomaly inspection with operator dashboards and an audit trail, by 30 September 2026, within a total budgeted cost of \vTBC{} notional and ₹0 cash. The expected benefit is consistent, traceable inspection decisions that can replace fatigue-prone manual checks and supply reviewed data for retraining.
